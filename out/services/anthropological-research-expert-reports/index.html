Anthropological research, native title support and expert reports

Thirteen years as a regional and senior anthropologist with the Northern Land Council and the Tiwi Land Council, plus ANU-CNTA native title research training, underpin a research practice that land councils, Aboriginal corporations, lawyers and government can rely on.

What is included

  • Native title connection research, genealogies and claim group descriptions
  • Land claim and Aboriginal Land Rights (NT) Act 1976 research, including section 19 lease and consent processes
  • Independent anthropological reports and peer reviews for lawyers, land councils and tribunals
  • Traditional Owner identification and succession advice for agreements, royalties and distributions
  • Historical, archival and museum collection research, including repatriation and provenance of cultural objects
  • Culturally informed advice to boards, corporations and government on governance and decision-making

Who it is for

Land councils and Native Title representative bodies, Aboriginal corporations and PBCs, law firms, NT and Commonwealth agencies, museums and research institutions.

Pricing

Research is quoted per brief, usually as a capped number of days with milestones.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & East Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Can you act as an expert witness?

Helen Haritos prepares independent anthropological reports to the standard expected of an expert and can give evidence where required. Each brief is assessed for conflicts before acceptance.

Do you work outside the Northern Territory?

The practice is NT-focused with capacity for the East Kimberley region of Western Australia, where Helen has lived and worked. International archival research has been undertaken in Europe for Tiwi cultural objects.

How is confidential cultural information handled?

Restricted information stays with the people it belongs to. Reports distinguish between what can be published and what must remain confidential, and data is stored and shared under agreed protocols.

Cultural awareness training, heritage inductions and ceremony facilitation

Most heritage incidents on construction sites happen because somebody did not know. A half-day induction built around the actual project, its Authority Certificate and the people whose Country it is on prevents most of them, and sets the tone for the relationship with Traditional Owners.

What is included

  • Project-specific cultural heritage and sacred site inductions for site crews, delivered on site or online
  • Cultural awareness sessions for project teams, boards and government staff, grounded in the Country the project is on
  • Facilitation of Welcome to Country, smoking ceremonies and site openings with the appropriate Traditional Owners, including payments and logistics
  • Protocols for working with Traditional Owners, cultural monitors and communities
  • Induction records and attendance registers for compliance files

Who it is for

Head contractors mobilising a Darwin or Top End site, developers launching a project, government agencies and corporate teams working on Aboriginal land or with Aboriginal organisations.

Pricing

Inductions and sessions are priced per session; ceremony facilitation is priced per event with Traditional Owner payments passed through at cost.

Frequently asked questions

How do we arrange a Welcome to Country in Darwin?

A Welcome to Country should be given by a Larrakia Traditional Owner. Connected2Country identifies the right person for the location and occasion, agrees the fee, and handles the logistics so that the event is respectful and the relationship is sound.

Is a cultural induction the same as a general cultural awareness course?

No. A general course explains culture in the abstract. A project induction tells your crew which areas are restricted, what the Authority Certificate conditions mean on the ground, who the Traditional Owners are and what to do if something is found. Both have a place; the induction is the one that protects the project.

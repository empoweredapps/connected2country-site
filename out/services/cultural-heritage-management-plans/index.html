Cultural Heritage Management Plans and CEMP heritage sections

Approvals come with conditions and the conditions have to be lived with for the life of the project. Connected2Country writes management plans that construction crews can actually follow, then provides the inductions, monitoring and inspections that show the conditions were met.

What is included

  • Cultural Heritage Management Plans tied to Authority Certificate conditions, Heritage Act approvals and Traditional Owner agreements
  • Cultural heritage sections and procedures for Construction Environmental Management Plans
  • Unexpected finds and ancestral remains procedures
  • Site induction content and delivery for crews and subcontractors
  • Cultural monitor programs with Traditional Owners during ground disturbance
  • Compliance inspections, audits and close-out reporting for superintendents and regulators

Who it is for

Civil head contractors and subcontractors on NT Government, Defence, utility and private development contracts; developers passing approval conditions down to contractors; environmental consultants preparing CEMPs who need the heritage component.

Pricing

Plans are fixed price for defined scopes. Inductions, monitoring and inspections are on an agreed day rate or per-visit price.

Available in Darwin, Palmerston & Rural · Top End · NT & East Kimberley.

Frequently asked questions

Does my CEMP need a cultural heritage section?

Almost always. NT Government, Defence and most private contract environmental clauses require the CEMP to address heritage and sacred sites, and any Authority Certificate or Heritage Act condition has to be translated into site controls. A generic paragraph will not satisfy a superintendent.

What happens if we find artefacts or remains during works?

Stop work in the immediate area, protect the find and notify the Heritage Branch (and police for suspected human remains). Your plan should say exactly who does what. Connected2Country responds to unexpected finds across the Darwin region and advises remotely elsewhere.

Can you work with our existing environmental consultant?

Yes. Connected2Country regularly provides the heritage component to environmental consultancies, including Bell Enviro, so that one integrated plan goes to the client.

Services

Traditional Owner and Native Title holder consultation

Anthropologist-led consultation with Larrakia and other Traditional Owners for developments in Darwin, Palmerston and the Top End.

AAPA Authority Certificate support and sacred site advice

Help obtaining and complying with Aboriginal Areas Protection Authority (AAPA) Authority Certificates in the NT: applications, custodian consultation, restricted work area interpretation and construction compliance..

Cultural heritage and cultural values assessments

Ethnographic and cultural values assessments, Heritage Act 2011 advice and EIS culture and heritage chapters for projects in Darwin, the Top End and the NT, prepared by an anthropologist and archaeologist..

Cultural Heritage Management Plans and CEMP heritage sections

Project-specific Cultural Heritage Management Plans (CHMPs) and the cultural heritage sections of Construction Environmental Management Plans for NT civil and development projects, with inductions, monitoring and inspections..

Anthropological research, native title support and expert reports

Consultant anthropologist for native title and land claim research, genealogies, connection reports, land council and ALRA matters and expert reports in the Northern Territory and East Kimberley..

Cultural awareness training, heritage inductions and ceremony facilitation

Project-specific cultural awareness sessions, sacred site and heritage inductions for construction crews, and facilitation of Welcome to Country and smoking ceremonies with Traditional Owners in Darwin and the Top End..

AI and digital services for cultural heritage

Responsible use of AI and digital tools in cultural heritage: AI-assisted desktop assessments and research, secure cultural knowledge recording and archives, and Indigenous Data Sovereignty advice for Traditional Owner groups and proponents in the NT..

Why proponents and consultancies use Connected2Country

Over 20 years on Country

More than two decades in NT Aboriginal land and heritage work, including senior anthropologist roles with the Northern Land Council and Tiwi Land Council. Helen knows how Traditional Owner decisions are made and how land councils, AAPA and the Heritage Branch assess what you submit.

Anthropology and archaeology

Both disciplines in one practitioner: the intangible values and the right people to speak, plus the archaeological places the Heritage Act protects.

Local and available

Darwin-based, so consultation happens when Traditional Owners are available, not when flights are. Remote-capable across the Top End and into the East Kimberley.

Modern tools, human judgement

AI handles the desktop reading, drafting and registers so that expert time goes into Country, people and the judgement calls. Faster and cheaper desktop work without cutting corners on the fieldwork.

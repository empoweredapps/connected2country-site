Traditional Owner consultation and cultural heritage across the Top End

Helen Haritos has over 20 years in Aboriginal land and heritage work in the Territory, including regional and senior anthropologist roles with the Northern Land Council and senior anthropologist with the Tiwi Land Council, working across the Top End on site surveys, land claims, mining and economic development consultations and Aboriginal Land Rights Act lease negotiations. That experience means Connected2Country knows the land council processes from the inside, understands how Traditional Owner decision-making works in remote communities and plans fieldwork around ceremony, sorry business and the Wet season.

Areas covered

Katherine and the Katherine River region, Pine Creek and Adelaide River, Kakadu and Jabiru, West Arnhem including Gunbalanya and Maningrida, East Arnhem including Nhulunbuy and Groote Eylandt, the Tiwi Islands (Wurrumiyanga, Pirlangimpi and Milikapiti), the Daly River and Wadeye, Batchelor and Litchfield, and the Victoria River District to Timber Creek.

Typical work in this region

  • Sacred site surveys and Traditional Owner consultation for mining, energy, infrastructure and tourism proposals on Aboriginal land
  • Section 19 lease and land use agreement consultations under the Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern Territory) Act 1976
  • Cultural heritage and cultural values assessments for EIS projects
  • Collection, archive and museum support for community keeping places, including the Tiwi Islands
  • Cultural heritage management and inductions for remote construction programs

Services available

  • Traditional Owner and Native Title holder consultation
  • AAPA Authority Certificate support and sacred site advice
  • Cultural heritage and cultural values assessments
  • Cultural Heritage Management Plans and CEMP heritage sections
  • Anthropological research, native title support and expert reports
  • Cultural awareness training, heritage inductions and ceremony facilitation
  • AI and digital services for cultural heritage

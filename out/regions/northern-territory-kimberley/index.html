Anthropological services across the Northern Territory and into the East Kimberley

For projects beyond the Top End, Connected2Country works with the relevant land council or Native Title representative body and local Traditional Owner organisations, and partners with regional specialists where that serves the client better. Helen has lived and worked in the East Kimberley and understands that heritage in Western Australia is governed by the Aboriginal Heritage Act 1972 as amended in 2023, with its section 18 consent process, the Aboriginal Cultural Heritage Inquiry System and the state's Aboriginal Heritage Survey Program, rather than the NT's AAPA and Heritage Act regime.

Areas covered

The whole of the Northern Territory including Tennant Creek, the Barkly, Alice Springs and Central Australia, and the East Kimberley region of Western Australia: Kununurra, Wyndham, Lake Argyle, the Ord River irrigation area, Halls Creek and the Great Northern Highway corridor.

Typical work in this region

  • Native title research and connection reports
  • Anthropological input to heritage surveys and section 18 applications in the East Kimberley
  • Traditional Owner consultation for cross-border infrastructure, agriculture and resources projects
  • Independent anthropological reports and peer reviews
  • AI and data governance advice to Aboriginal corporations and ranger groups

Services available

  • Traditional Owner and Native Title holder consultation
  • AAPA Authority Certificate support and sacred site advice
  • Cultural heritage and cultural values assessments
  • Cultural Heritage Management Plans and CEMP heritage sections
  • Anthropological research, native title support and expert reports
  • Cultural awareness training, heritage inductions and ceremony facilitation
  • AI and digital services for cultural heritage

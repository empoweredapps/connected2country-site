Traditional Owner consultation and cultural heritage in Darwin, Palmerston and the rural area

Helen has lived in Darwin all her life and has worked with Larrakia and neighbouring Traditional Owners through the Northern Land Council, the NT Government and now C2C. Being local matters more than it sounds: meetings happen when people are available rather than when a consultant can get a flight, and C2C knows how Darwin approvals, AAPA, the Heritage Branch and the Development Consent Authority actually run.

Areas covered

Darwin city and northern suburbs, Lee Point, Casuarina and the coastal reserves, East Arm, Berrimah and Winnellie, Darwin International Airport and RAAF Base precincts, Palmerston and its growth suburbs, Holtze, Howard Springs, Humpty Doo, Coolalinga, Berry Springs, Noonamah, Virginia, Bees Creek, the Litchfield municipality, Cox Peninsula, Dundee and the Finniss River country.

Typical work in this region

  • Traditional Owner consultation for residential subdivisions and master-planned developments in the northern suburbs and Palmerston growth areas
  • AAPA Authority Certificate support for infrastructure, utility and Defence precinct works
  • Cultural heritage sections of CEMPs for civil contractors on NT Government and council contracts
  • Welcome to Country and ceremony facilitation for project launches and openings
  • Desktop and field heritage assessments for industrial land at East Arm, Berrimah and Winnellie and rural subdivisions in Litchfield

Services available

  • Traditional Owner and Native Title holder consultation
  • AAPA Authority Certificate support and sacred site advice
  • Cultural heritage and cultural values assessments
  • Cultural Heritage Management Plans and CEMP heritage sections
  • Anthropological research, native title support and expert reports
  • Cultural awareness training, heritage inductions and ceremony facilitation
  • AI and digital services for cultural heritage

About Connected2Country and Helen Haritos

I am an anthropologist and archaeologist of Aboriginal descent. I have lived in Darwin all my life, and for most of the last twenty years I worked for Traditional Owners inside Northern Territory land councils and government before setting up Connected2Country.

Experience

  • Senior Anthropologist, Tiwi Land Council (2022–2024): engagement with Tiwi Islanders, section 19 Land Rights Act lease negotiations, formation of the Tiwi cultural committee, sacred site surveys with AAPA, curation of artefact collections, and a flora and fauna clearance survey at the Osprey crash site.
  • Senior Anthropologist, Northern Land Council (2017–2021): research and review of land claim findings, consultations with Traditional Owners, anthropological reports, genealogies and sacred site records.
  • Regional Anthropologist, Northern Land Council (2008–2017): site surveys and clearances, Traditional Owner consultations, genealogies, economic development and mining project consultations, and native title case support across the Top End.
  • Researcher, Archaeology of the Tiwi Islands Project (2024): international archival research in Leiden, Rome and Paris on Tiwi cultural objects.
  • Archivist and research support, Patakijiyali Museum, Wurrumiyanga (2024–2025): cataloguing and preparing photographs, film and literature for digitisation with Tiwi women.
  • NT Government, Department of Primary Industry, Fisheries and Mines (2003–2008): ALRA Unit project officer advising on exploration and mining on Aboriginal land and compliance under the Land Rights and Native Title Acts, then Senior Titles Officer, Petroleum.
  • Archaeologist, EcOz Environmental Services (2003): archaeological surveys for the Trans Territory Pipeline corridor.
  • Anthropologist and archaeologist with Bell Enviro (2023–present): cultural heritage consultation, compliance inspections and audits, AAPA clearance consultations and ethno-ecology advice for civil and development projects.

Qualifications and memberships

  • BA (Hons) Archaeology, Charles Darwin University
  • Graduate Diploma of Languages (Hellenic Studies) and Certificate of Arts (Hellenic Studies), Charles Darwin University
  • Native Title Guided Research Placement, ANU Centre for Native Title Anthropology, with Dr Julie Finlayson and Professor Nicholas Peterson
  • Introduction to Maritime Archaeology (AIMA/NAS)
  • Member, Australian Anthropological Society; American Anthropological Association; Archaeological and Anthropological Society of Victoria; World Archaeological Congress (Independent Scholar)

Community

Chairperson of Friends of East Point Inc (2007–2019), Deputy President of University Women of Australia NT Branch (2004–2008), and long involvement with the Historical Society of the Northern Territory. Helen has also lived and worked in the East Kimberley.

How I work

It is a one-person practice and I do the work myself. On bigger programs I bring in archaeologists, interpreters, cultural advisers and GIS help I have worked with before, and I team up with Bell Enviro when a project needs the environmental and heritage approvals handled together. I use AI for desktop research, drafting and record-keeping because it saves clients money; I do not use it as a substitute for going on Country or for my own judgement.

I acknowledge the Larrakia people as Traditional Owners of the Darwin region, and the Traditional Owners of all the Country I work on.
